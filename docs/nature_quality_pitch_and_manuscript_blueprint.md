# Nature-quality pitch and manuscript blueprint

Status: synchronized with the 2026-09-22 manuscript draft. `SUPPORTED`,
`BOUNDED`, and `PLACEHOLDER` are editorial claim classes, not aesthetic labels.

## One-sentence editorial proposition

Formal verification becomes scientifically useful when it is used to expose
which physical assumptions make a mechanism identifiable, and when every proof
is coupled to a fidelity gate and a discriminating empirical observation.

## Article arc

### 1. Prediction is not mechanism identification

Establish the practical materials-science problem. High-fidelity simulation,
spectral fits and macroscopic transport trends are valuable but map many-to-one
from mechanism to observable. The scientific gap is therefore not another
predictor but an auditable way to identify what a prediction leaves unresolved.

### 2. A certificate-mediated closure workflow

Introduce Fig. 1 and the responsibility split: physics defines admissible
hypotheses; AI enumerates and structures them; the certificate freezes scope and
provenance; Lean proves only encoded consequences; numerical/domain checks test
witness admissibility; empirical observations decide the material mechanism.

### 3. Structural non-identifiability in NASICON

Show why a scalar barrier summary does not determine an event network. Report
the frozen kMC gates, failed convergence and bounded reversed ordering. Make the
promotion ceiling visible in the first result paragraph and caption.

### 4. Practical non-identifiability in EIS/NLEIS

Separate held-out spectral adequacy from stable electrode allocation. Report
the 30-spectrum result and avoid the stronger conclusion that NLEIS is generally
uninformative.

### 5. Specification non-identifiability in polymer transport

Decompose direct observations, inverse-model outputs and causal narrative. Use
the Wang composite null as the current auditable result. Keep the negative-
transference case prospective until the temporal seal and fidelity audit pass.

### 6. Cross-case synthesis

Compare three failure modes with a common evidence matrix. The synthesis claim
is about where inference stops, not that the three physical systems share one
mechanism.

### 7. Discussion and closure roadmap

Explain that a failed promotion gate can be a positive scientific result because
it preserves the distinction between prediction and identification. End with
the exact experiments needed to promote each claim.

## Editorial decisions already made

- The paper leads with the materials inference problem, not Lean or a benchmark.
- Formal proof is presented as a conditional component of a larger evidence
  chain, never as proof that a material mechanism is true.
- Failed preregistered gates are retained and interpreted; they are not rerun
  away.
- The polymer case distinguishes Wang's segmental-decoupling audit from the
  prospective negative-transference study.
- Main-text numbers are traceable to versioned JSON; source claims and legal
  data boundaries remain in the repository.
